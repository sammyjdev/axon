## What to look for

Findings the same-family reviewers would be least likely to catch. Do not
re-litigate style or re-run the gate - both already passed. Prefer: correctness
under inputs the tests do not generate, security consequences of the chosen
approach, and assumptions the diff makes about the environment.

Three specific things worth an independent lens:

1. **Was register the right call over cut?** The whole PR rests on that
   judgment. If the evidence chain above is weak, say so - the opposite outcome
   (formally cutting it and naming it in the cut list) was a legitimate reading
   of the issue.
2. **Is removing the allowlist outright too aggressive?** The parity guard is
   now `assert missing == set()` with no exclusion mechanism at all. That is
   strictly stronger today, but it means any future intentional pb-only command
   fails the guard with no sanctioned way to declare intent. Is a justified
   exclusion mechanism worth keeping, or is forcing the conversation the point?
3. **The behavioral test monkeypatches `pb._RUNTIME` with a
   `SimpleNamespace(vault_root=...)`.** It passes, but it assumes `index_vault`'s
   `--dry-run` path touches nothing on `_RUNTIME` except `vault_root`. If that
   path ever widens, the fake silently becomes wrong rather than failing loudly.
   Is there a sturdier local idiom in tests/cli/test_pb_cli.py?
